About

I recently received my Ph.D. under the supervision of Prof. Tae Hyun Kim. My research focuses on restoring and enhancing images and videos degraded by sub-optimal capture conditions, with the broader goal of turning any capture into a good photo. I approach this problem from three directions: designing task-specific network architectures, formulating restoration as an inverse problem, and leveraging generative models.

What makes a photo good, however, depends on who is looking at it. While much of my work has focused on visual quality for human viewers, I am increasingly interested in enhancement for machines: improving images so that downstream vision models can perceive and act on them more reliably. If you are interested in collaboration, feel free to contact me!
